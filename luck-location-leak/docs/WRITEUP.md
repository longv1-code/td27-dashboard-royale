# Luck, Location, or Leak? — write-up

**Question.** The GM wants to replace The Gilded Ace's 10 lowest-earning slot machines (fictional casino, synthetic data). We tested three explanations first.

**Luck.** From each machine's bet sizes and payout variance, we measured actual win minus theo in standard deviations. Across 310 machines the spread is what chance predicts: 20 beyond ±2σ against 14 expected. Most low earners are just unlucky.

**Location.** Nine of the ten sit in Back Forty, which earns 67% of the floor's theo per available hour for the same game mix. When renovation closed its walkway from the main entrance (Mar 3–Apr 6), Back Forty's play fell 31% relative to the rest of the floor, then recovered. Access drives it. Three of the ten were not on the floor all period: two were retired and one was installed in May.

**Leak.** Hit rate is far less noisy than hold, so we tested it against each game's published hit probability. One machine, M-1158, hits on 38.6% of spins against 31.5% published (z = 124, already 8.6σ on day one), and held 3.0% against a configured 10%. It is a lead for a paytable inspection, not evidence of wrongdoing.

**Fair ranking.** Comparing theo per available hour with identical games in the same zone, only 7 machines floor-wide are truly weak, and none of them is on the manager's list.

**Downtime** cost about $99k of theo (0.42%), 55% of it from Ridgeline cabinets, peaking 8 p.m.–midnight. A no-show on a one-tech shift stretches the repair wait from 0.1 to 4.7 hours.

**Players** (carded only) wagered $163.8M and netted −$15.6M, close to the $15.5M theo. The top 5% produce 52% of theo; 16% finished ahead. **Comps:** on May 1 points per comp dollar halved in every tier. Redemptions didn't move, but the unredeemed point balance is now worth $7.6M instead of $3.3M.

**Model.** A log-linear weekday + known-event forecast, frozen on April 30, scored $49k MAE on May–June against $218k for a constant mean. Used as a counterfactual, it shows the February tournament added about $72k of theo.

**$100k plan (July).** Inspect M-1158 ($1,200), add wayfinding for Back Forty ($7,500; break-even lift 2.9%), and run one tournament weekend ($15,000). That spends $23,700 for an expected $61k of theo (range $15k–$107k), and we keep $76,300. Each action has a pre-registered measurement on the dashboard. Replacing the bottom 10, adding technicians and preventive maintenance all fail break-even within 30 days.
